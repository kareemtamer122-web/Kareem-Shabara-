# ☕ Coffee Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The Coffee Sales Dashboard is a data visualization project designed to analyze coffee sales data and present meaningful insights through interactive charts.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze coffee sales data.
* Track sales trends over time.
* Compare different coffee types.
* Explore sales distribution by country.
* Use interactive filters to explore the data.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

* **Total Sales Over Time:** Displays sales trends across months and years.
* **Sales By Country:** Shows sales distribution across different countries.
* **Coffee Types:** Compares Arabica, Excelsa, Liberica, and Robusta.
* **Interactive Filters:** Filters data by date, size, roast type, and loyalty card.
